About SAMANA

Connecting travelers with authentic cultural experiences, local communities, and the stories that make every place special.

Our Story

Experience India Beyond the Tourist Path

SAMANA was created to help travelers discover the real culture of India through meaningful experiences with local people and communities.

Instead of simply visiting a destination, travelers can connect with local traditions, food, art, homes, farms, celebrations, and everyday life.

Our goal is to make cultural travel more personal, respectful, and memorable while creating opportunities for local hosts to share what makes their communities unique.

Our Mission
